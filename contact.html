Common questions

Do you service apartments?

Yes. Most visits are flats. Tell us the tower and whether the association needs a gate pass.

What should we do before you arrive?

Clear the floor edge in the affected room and tell us about pets or children. Bed bug jobs include a longer prep list.

Are the products suitable around children and pets?

We plan the visit around them and tell you when a room can be used again. Ask for the product name before work starts.

How soon can you come?

Same day when the schedule allows. Otherwise the next open morning or evening slot.

Can pest control and cleaning be booked together?

Yes. We time the cleaning so it does not wash a fresh treatment away.

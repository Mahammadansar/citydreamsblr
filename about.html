A Bengaluru team for pests and hygiene

City Dreams treats the flat or shop in front of us. We do not sell one spray for every pest, and we do not start until you know what the visit includes.

Our work

Built around apartments, monsoon, and busy kitchens

Most of our calls start the same way. Cockroaches in a kitchen that was cleaned yesterday. Termites in a door frame after the rains. Mosquitoes from a balcony pot or an AC tray. Pigeons on a ledge the neighbour also uses.

We inspect that path, treat the source, and tell you what will bring the pest back if it stays: open snacks, a wet sink overnight, cardboard behind the fridge, or a gap the building still needs to close.

On the day

What you can expect

A look first

We identify the pest and the rooms involved before any product is opened.

A plain explanation

You hear where we will work, what to move, and when the room can be used again.

Targeted treatment

Baits, dusts, proofing, or cleaning aimed at the harbour, not a fog of the whole home.

Aftercare

A short note: what not to wash for a few days, and what habit is feeding the pest.

How we choose methods

Household safety is part of the plan

Tell us about children, pets, aquariums, pregnancy, or a kitchen that stays open. We time the visit around that. Ask to see the product name and the re-entry guidance before work starts. If a gap belongs to the building, we will say so instead of pretending a spray can close it.

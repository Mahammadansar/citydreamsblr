Cleaning that works with pest control

Dirt, grease, and damp are what cockroaches, flies, and ants use. Book cleaning on its own, or on the same day as a treatment.

01

Deep home cleaning

A full pass of an apartment or house: floors, skirting, fans, switches, windows you can reach, and the corners a weekly mop misses. Useful before a festival, after the monsoon, or when a flat has been locked up.

  • Rooms, balcony, and utility area
  • Inside empty cabinets if you have cleared them
  • Can be paired with cockroach or ant treatment

Kitchen and bathroom hygiene

Grease on the chimney side, the gap beside the stove, floor traps, and bathroom joints. This is the cleaning that actually changes pest pressure, because it removes the food and the film they live on.

  • Chimney exterior, tiles, sink, and slab
  • Bathroom fittings, floor traps, and grout
  • Best booked with flies or cockroach control
